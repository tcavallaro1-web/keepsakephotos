Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Template text. Have this page reviewed by a qualified attorney before launch.

This policy explains how KC Clutterfree, LLC (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the Keepsake Photos desktop app for Windows (the “App”) and this website (the “Site”).

The short version

  • Keepsake Photos runs on your PC. Your photos, videos, and drive contents are processed locally and are not sent to us.
  • Photos leave your PC only when you connect a third-party photo service and choose to send them there.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information processed on your PC

To organize your library, the App reads file names, folders, sizes, dates, and photo metadata (such as the date a photo was taken) on the drives you select. This processing happens on your computer. We do not receive copies of your photos or a list of your files.

Third-party photo services

If you connect a service such as Google Photos, Google Drive, iCloud, Shutterfly, OneDrive, Dropbox, Amazon Photos, or Flickr, the App communicates directly with that service on your instruction. Your use of those services is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. You can disconnect a service at any time.

Purchases

Payments are handled by PayPal or Square. We never see or store your full card details. From your order we receive your name, email address, the edition you purchased, and transaction details, which we use to deliver your activation code, provide support, keep business records, and prevent fraud.

License activation

When you enter an activation code, the App may store the code, the name and email on the license, and a device identifier so that the license can be verified and the number of activated computers kept within your edition’s limit.

Support requests

If you email us, we use your message and contact details to respond and to improve the App. Please don’t send photos or other sensitive files unless we ask for them to resolve your issue.

This website

The Site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability.

Retention and security

We keep order and support records only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect them. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to legal record-keeping obligations. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the CCPA or GDPR. Contact us at support@keepsakephotos.net.

Children

The App and Site are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
