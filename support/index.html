Install in four steps

  1. Download the installer from the download section on your Windows PC.
  2. Run the installer: double-click Keepsake-Photos-Setup-1.3.11.exe. If SmartScreen appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open Keepsake Photos from the Windows Start menu and accept the terms.
  4. Organize: use every feature free for 3 days, with scans of up to 1,000 photos. Enter your serial to scan the rest.

Common questions

Windows says it protected my PC

SmartScreen can show this for new apps. If you downloaded Keepsake Photos from this site, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Check that your download is genuine

In PowerShell, open your Downloads folder and run Get-FileHash .\Keepsake-Photos-Setup-1.3.11.exe. The result should be 6ab62d01c197da9c00fd7bff15b6ba01a93b8b00e28fec880784b556bf166d4b.

Entering your serial

In the app, open the ··· menu, choose Serial, and paste your serial into Unlock Keepsake Photos.

I bought a serial but haven’t received it

Check your spam or promotions folder for an email from us. If it isn’t there, email support@keepsakephotos.net with your PayPal or Square receipt.

My serial is not accepted

Copy and paste the serial exactly as it appears in your email, with no extra spaces. If it still isn’t accepted, write to us with your receipt.

Moving to a new PC

Your serial covers the number of computers in your edition: Home 1, Family 3, Studio 5. Email us if you need to move a license to a new computer.
