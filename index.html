Features

Built for a drawer full of drives.

Not another folder copier. Keepsake counts what you have, shows you what it found, and waits for you to say go.

  • Inventory

    See every drive at a glance: its size, free space, and how many photos and videos it holds.

  • Clean out the clutter

    Icons, Windows sample pictures, and thumbnail caches are set aside before anything is filed.

  • Compare duplicates

    See look-alike photos side by side. Keep the best one, or clear a whole batch at once.

    Inventory, clean up, remove duplicates, and file, in a single run you start yourself.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Keepsake Photos do?

Keepsake Photos is a Windows photo organizer. It takes stock of every drive you connect, sets aside clutter such as icons and thumbnail caches, helps you remove duplicates, and files your originals into year folders on the drive you choose. It can also read old and failing disks and send your best photos to the photo services you already use.

How does the free trial work?

Download Keepsake Photos and use every feature free for 3 days, with no card required. During the trial you can scan up to two drives, change up to 25 photos, and upload up to 10. After 3 days, an activation code unlocks everything for good.

Is it a subscription?

No. Your activation code is a one-time purchase that unlocks Keepsake Photos for life: Home is $29.95, Family is $37.45, and Studio is $149.

Will it move or delete anything without asking?

No. Keepsake counts and previews first. Nothing moves until you press Start, and Pause and Stop stay on screen the whole time. Drive Mechanic never formats a disk.

Do my photos leave my PC?

Only if you choose. Keepsake works on your PC and your drives. Photos are uploaded only when you connect a photo service, such as Google Photos, iCloud, Shutterfly, or OneDrive, and choose to send your keepers there.

How do I pay, and how do I get my activation code?

Pay securely with PayPal, or by card through Square checkout (which also supports Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Cash App). This site never sees your card details. Your activation code is emailed to you after purchase. Paste it into the app to unlock everything.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit. Keepsake Photos is not available for Mac, iPhone, iPad, or Android.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is that normal?

Windows SmartScreen can show this notice for new apps it has not seen often. If you downloaded Keepsake Photos from this site, choose More info and then Run anyway.
