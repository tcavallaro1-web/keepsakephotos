FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Keepsake Photos do?

Keepsake Photos is a Windows photo organizer. It scans the drives and folders you choose and counts every photo and video, finds exact and near-duplicates for you to review side by side, and sorts your originals into year folders on the destination you pick. It can also copy or consolidate photos into one place and turn sideways JPEGs upright.

How does the free trial work?

Download Keepsake Photos and use every feature free for 3 days, with no card required. The trial scans up to 1,000 photos. When you’re ready, enter a serial to scan the rest and keep Keepsake Photos for life.

Is it a subscription?

No. A serial is a one-time purchase that unlocks Keepsake Photos for life: Home is $29.95, Family is $37.45, and Studio is $149.

Will it move or delete anything without asking?

No. Scanning only reads your photos; nothing is moved. Changes happen only after you choose an action and confirm it. Duplicates you remove go to the Windows Recycle Bin, and Undo can put them back. Erasing a drive asks you to type a confirmation first.

Do my photos leave my PC?

No. Keepsake Photos works on your PC with the drives and folders you choose. Your photos are never sent to us.

How do I pay, and how do I get my serial?

Pay securely by card through Square (which also supports Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Cash App) or with PayPal. This site never sees your card details. Your serial is emailed to you after purchase. In the app, open the ··· menu, choose Serial, and paste it in.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit. Keepsake Photos is not available for Mac, iPhone, iPad, or Android.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is that normal?

Windows SmartScreen can show this notice for new apps it hasn’t seen often. If you downloaded the installer from this site, choose More info and then Run anyway. You can also check the download against the SHA-256 published in the download section.
